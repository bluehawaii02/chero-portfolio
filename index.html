Services

What i offer

Whether you need a one-off deep-dive or an ongoing analytics partner, here's what I do.

01

Data Analysis & Insights

I dig into your data to answer the questions that matter,what's driving churn, where revenue is leaking, or why a metric shifted. You get a clear answer, not just a spreadsheet.

  1. Exploratory Analysis
  2. Root Cause Report
  3. Trend Identification
02

Dashboard & Reporting

I build dashboards your team will actually open,pulling from multiple sources, updated automatically, and designed so the right number is always visible at a glance.

  1. Tableau / Looker
  2. KPI Tracking
  3. Automated reports
03

Predictive Modelling

When you need to get ahead of what's coming, churn prediction, demand forecasting, risk scoring ,I build models in Python that give your team an edge, not just historical hindsight.

  1. Churn prediction
  2. Forecasting
  3. Segmentation
04

Data Cleaning & Pipeline SetUp

Messy data, broken pipelines, or no pipeline at all, I get your data flowing reliably from source to insight so your team isn't starting from scratch every time.

  1. SQL/dbt
  2. ETL set up
  3. Data quality audits
05

Data Strategy Consulting Services

Not sure where to start with data, or whether the data you're collecting is even the right data? I work with founders and leaders to figure out what to measure and why.

  1. Tool Selection
  2. Team Workshop
  3. Metrics framework
06

A/B Testing and Experimentation

I design and analyse experiments so your product decisions are grounded in evidence, from setting up the test correctly to interpreting the results without fooling yourself.

  1. Test Design
  2. Result Reporting
  3. Statistical analysis
